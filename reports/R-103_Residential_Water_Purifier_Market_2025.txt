EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: RESIDENTIAL WATER PURIFIER MARKET 2025
Consumer demand for residential water filtration has surged worldwide in response to heightened public awareness of tap water contaminants, including per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS), microplastics, disinfection byproducts, and heavy metals. Point-of-use (POU) countertop and under-sink filtration systems represent the fastest-growing residential appliance category, driven by direct-to-consumer digital channels and subscription-based filter cartridge replacement business models.

TECHNOLOGY SEGMENTATION: REVERSE OSMOSIS VS. UV VS. GRAVITY
Filtration technologies vary significantly across consumer price tiers and water hardness profiles:
1. Reverse Osmosis (RO) Systems: Account for the highest value share due to multi-stage filtration capabilities that remove up to 99% of total dissolved solids (TDS). Recent product innovations have focused on improving water recovery efficiency from traditional 1:3 ratios to 1:1 permeate-to-waste ratios.
2. Ultraviolet (UV) Disinfection: Primary technology in municipal supply zones with microbiological hazards but moderate mineral content.
3. Gravity and Pitcher Filters: High unit volume in emerging economies where low initial capital expense is paramount.
