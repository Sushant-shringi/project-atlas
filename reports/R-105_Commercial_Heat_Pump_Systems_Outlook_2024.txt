REFRIGERANT TRANSITION AND OPERATIONAL ECONOMICS
Regulatory phases under the EU F-Gas Regulation and the US AIM Act are compelling HVAC equipment manufacturers to replace hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs) with ultra-low global warming potential (GWP) alternatives. While transition engineering requires re-designed compressors and enhanced flammability mitigation, commercial building operators benefit from 30% to 50% reductions in primary operating energy costs.
